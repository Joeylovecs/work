===================Question===================
in what years were the total deaths more than in 1940/41 but less than in 1939/40?
===================Text===================
```python
import pandas as pd

data = {
    "1939/40": [360000, 75000, 69000, None, None, None, 504000],
    "1940/41": [None, 100000, 210000, 42000, None, None, 352000],
    "1941/42": [None, 116000, 220000, 71000, None, None, 407000],
    "1942/43": [None, 133000, 266000, 142000, None, None, 541000],
    "1943/44": [None, 82000, 381000, 218000, None, None, 681000],
    "1944/45": [183000, None, None, None, 100000, None, 270000],
}
df = pd.DataFrame(data, index=["Direct War Losses","Murdered","Deaths In Prisons & Camps",
                               "Deaths Outside of Prisons & Camps","Murdered in Eastern Regions",
                               "Deaths other countries","Total"])
total = df.loc["Total"]
years = [c for c in df.columns if total["1940/41"] < total[c] < total["1939/40"]]
answer = years
print(answer)
```
