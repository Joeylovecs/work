===================Question===================
what company has the longest tenure as kit manufacturer?
===================Text===================
```python
# Find the kit manufacturer with the longest tenure
# First, extract the start year from the Year column
df['Start_Year'] = df['Year'].str.split('–').str[0].astype(int)

# Group by kit manufacturer and calculate tenure as max year - min year + 1
manufacturer_tenure = df.groupby('Kit Manufacturer')['Start_Year'].agg(['min', 'max'])
manufacturer_tenure['tenure_years'] = manufacturer_tenure['max'] - manufacturer_tenure['min'] + 1

# Find the manufacturer with the maximum tenure
longest_tenure = manufacturer_tenure['tenure_years'].max()
longest_manufacturer = manufacturer_tenure[manufacturer_tenure['tenure_years'] == longest_tenure].index[0]

answer = longest_manufacturer
```
